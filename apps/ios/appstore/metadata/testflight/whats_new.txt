New in this build (0.3):
• GPS: openhead watch gps. iOS asks for location access the first time; the location goes only to the paired controller on your network.
• Barometer, magnetometer and compass: openhead watch env. iOS asks for Motion & Fitness the first time (for the barometer).
• The HUD can show {lat}, {lon}, {alt} and {pressure} while those streams run.
• The compass heading now follows the screen as the phone is mounted in landscape.

Please try on an iPhone 12: openhead watch gps -n 3 near a window, openhead watch env -n 5 while turning the phone (heading should change, pressure stay near your local value), and run scenarios/camera.yaml again to check that 0.2 still works.
