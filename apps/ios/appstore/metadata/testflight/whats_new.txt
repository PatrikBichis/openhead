New in this build (0.2):
• Camera view with a HUD: openhead camera hunter --follow. Four HUD looks: hunter, camcorder, boot, flight. iOS asks for camera permission the first time.
• Finds people: boxes around faces, the nearest is locked, and the eyes follow it (--follow). openhead watch faces prints where they are.
• The HUD shows battery, temperature, compass heading, tilt and the robot's own lines: openhead hud --set task="SAY HI" --line "TASK {task}".
• ARKit position: openhead mode sensing, then openhead watch pose. Move the phone around and see the position change.

Please try: run scenarios/camera.yaml (openhead run scenarios/camera.yaml) while standing in front of the phone, then check that the boxes sit on faces, the eyes follow you, the compass turns with the phone, and the pose moves when you carry it.
