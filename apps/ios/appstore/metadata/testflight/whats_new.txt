New in this build (0.5): a cockpit to watch and drive the head from your computer.
• Camera stream: a paired controller can ask for a small, low-frame-rate video of what the camera sees (front camera in capture mode, back camera in sensing). Frames are never stored. The face shows "Camera shared" in the top corner as long as anyone watches.
• On Pro models with LiDAR (optional; nothing changes on an iPhone 12): distance at points of the back camera's view, and a 3D mesh of the room while a controller asks for it.
• Status reports how many clients watch the camera.

Please try on an iPhone 12:
openhead cockpit --device <address>   (opens a dashboard in the browser)
Switch the mode to capture or sensing in the Mode card and check that the Camera card shows video and the phone shows "Camera shared"; close the page and check that the marker goes away. Try the Speak card's examples, the Expression card, and the sensor cards. Tell us how smooth the video is and whether the phone gets warm.
