New in this build:
• Subtitles on top of anything: openhead subtitle "Hello" --mouth. Use --mode append to stream an answer word by word; the newest three lines stay on screen.
• Video: openhead show clip.mp4 --overlay face-corner plays a file from your computer; the face comes back when it ends.
• Images now include SVG and animated GIF.
• The face fills the whole screen, edge to edge.
• Screenshots during a video show the frame instead of black.

Please try: run scenarios/display.yaml and scenarios/smoke.yaml (openhead run ...) and check that video plays smoothly, subtitles are easy to read from a distance, and the mouth moves with the text.
