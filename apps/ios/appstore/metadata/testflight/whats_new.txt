New in this build (0.4): the head talks and listens.
• Speaking: the head speaks with its own voice (Apple's on-device voices). The mouth moves with the sound, the words show as subtitles, and cues in the text change the expression in the right place: "[happy] Why did the robot go on holiday? [pause 0.5] It needed to recharge! [laugh]".
• Listening: speech to text with the microphone, on the device for English. iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time. The face looks attentive while you talk and thoughtful after.
• The head does not hear itself: listening pauses while it speaks, with echo cancellation.
• get_faces right after a mode change returns real faces.

Please try on an iPhone 12:
openhead say "[happy] Hello! [laugh] [sad 0.5] Goodbye." --device <address>
openhead listen --partial --device <address>   (talk, pause, see the text)
Or with an AI agent through MCP: say and listen in a loop. Tell us how natural the voice sounds, how well it understands you, and how fast it answers.
