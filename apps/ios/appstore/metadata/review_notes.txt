OpenHead turns the phone into the face of a robot. The app is driven over the local Wi-Fi by a robot's computer, a script or an AI agent; on its own it shows an animated face.

How to review it without a robot:
1. Open the app: an animated cartoon face appears (it blinks and looks around).
2. Long-press the face for 1.5 seconds to open Settings (name, port, theme when offline, dev mode, paired controllers).
3. To drive it, install the free open-source tools on a Mac on the same Wi-Fi (Python, uv): uv add "openhead @ git+https://github.com/PatrikBichis/openhead@v0.5.0#subdirectory=python", then run "openhead discover" and "openhead pair --device <address shown in Settings>". The app shows a 6-digit code; type it. Then try "openhead emotion happy", "openhead subtitle Hello --mouth", "openhead camera hunter --follow", "openhead watch gps -n 3", "openhead watch env -n 3", "openhead say \"[happy] Hello! [laugh]\"", "openhead listen --partial" (speak, then pause), and "openhead cockpit", which opens a dashboard in the browser with the camera, the sensors and a box for what the head should say.

Camera: used only when a paired controller asks for the camera view or face tracking; iOS asks for permission first. Images are processed on the device and are never stored. They are sent only as a small, low-frame-rate stream to a paired controller that subscribes to the camera stream (for example the cockpit), on the local network, and the face shows "Camera shared" the whole time. Location (While Using) and Motion & Fitness: asked only when a paired controller subscribes to the gps or env stream; readings go only to that controller on the local network and are not stored. Microphone and Speech Recognition: asked only when a paired controller starts listening (speech.listen); the recognised text goes only to that controller, audio is not stored, and recognition runs on the device when the language supports it (otherwise iOS uses Apple's speech recognition). Listening pauses while the head speaks and stops when the controller disconnects. Local Network permission is needed for controllers to connect. No account, no login, no data collection.
