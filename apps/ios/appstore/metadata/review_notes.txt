OpenHead turns the phone into the face of a robot. The app is driven over the local Wi-Fi by a robot's computer, a script or an AI agent; on its own it shows an animated face.

How to review it without a robot:
1. Open the app: an animated cartoon face appears (it blinks and looks around).
2. Long-press the face for 1.5 seconds to open Settings (name, port, theme when offline, dev mode, paired controllers).
3. To drive it, install the free open-source tools on a Mac on the same Wi-Fi (Python, uv): uv add "openhead @ git+https://github.com/PatrikBichis/openhead@v0.3.0#subdirectory=python", then run "openhead discover" and "openhead pair --device <address shown in Settings>". The app shows a 6-digit code; type it. Then try "openhead emotion happy", "openhead subtitle Hello --mouth", "openhead camera hunter --follow", "openhead watch gps -n 3", "openhead watch env -n 3".

Camera: used only when a paired controller asks for the camera view or face tracking; iOS asks for permission first. Images are processed on the device and are never stored or sent. Location (While Using) and Motion & Fitness: asked only when a paired controller subscribes to the gps or env stream; readings go only to that controller on the local network and are not stored. Local Network permission is needed for controllers to connect. No account, no login, no data collection.
